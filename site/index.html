FAQ

Asked, answered.

Do I need root?

No. If your phone runs the official TikTok, it runs TikTok MI.

Can it sit beside the official app?

No — same package name, so uninstall stock first. Account, drafts and data live on TikTok’s servers and survive the switch.

How do updates work?

The mod checks releases itself and offers the APK. Nothing installs silently — Android always asks first.

Is there an iPhone version?

No, Android only.

Something looks off — how do I report it?

Settings → TikTok MI → diary at the bottom: what the mod saw, copyable in one tap. Send it with your account ID to @dkramochka.
